## 1. Where files live
All active business records, price lists, invoices, and operational documents live in company-owned **Shared Drives** so the business retains ownership when staff change. Personal **My Drive** folders are restricted to working drafts only and must be moved into the relevant Shared Drive once finalized.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
External sharing is restricted to approved partner domains and requires explicit manager approval. Public "Anyone with the link" sharing is disabled on Shared Drives, and external viewers cannot download, print, or copy restricted folders.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active staff with assigned company Google Workspace accounts may sign in. **2-Step Verification (2SV)** is mandatory for every account, and mobile devices accessing company mail or Drive must enforce a screen lock under basic endpoint management.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
We keep customer invoices, supplier receipts, signed contracts, payroll summaries, and tax records in the Finance Shared Drive and monthly offline archive. Active records remain in Shared Drives for at least **to be checked** years (statutory tax retention period to be confirmed with the accountant), while deleted files remain in Shared Drive Trash for 30 days (plus 25 days admin recovery window).

## 5. The monthly export
On the last working day of each month, the designated admin runs a Google Takeout / Drive export of core Shared Drive folders and finance records. The encrypted `.zip` archive is stored on an offline external drive kept in a locked office cabinet, with a copy verified for readability.

## 6. Who holds admin
Two trusted leaders hold Super Admin access (the Owner and one designated backup admin), while daily staff use standard non-admin accounts. Each admin keeps printed 2SV backup codes on paper in a secure physical location—never stored in plain text on a shared computer.

## 7. When someone leaves
When a staff member leaves, the admin follows a fixed sequence: (1) reset password and revoke sign-in cookies, (2) remove the user from all Groups and Shared Drives, (3) transfer ownership of their My Drive files to their manager, (4) suspend the user account, and (5) run a final export if required before later account deletion.
